Hi. I’m Kaustubh, a junior at IIT Roorkee studying Electrical Engineering.  
I am a curious undergrad researcher working mainly on **probabilistic modelling**, **mechanistic interpretability**, **foundation models**, and a few projects in generative modelling.

I’m working in the [P-square lab](https://psquare-lab.github.io/Team/) with Prof. Parikshit. Our recent work focuses on scaling prior-fitted networks for improved few-shot uncertainty estimation, using a new attention mechanism inspired by Gaussian Processes. ([Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20950), [Code](https://github.com/PSquare-Lab/DVA-PFN)).  
We are also building a foundation model for amortized kernel hyperparameter discovery.

Mechanistic interpretability (something I really care about):  
I lead a small project with friends in the [Data Science Group](https://dsgiitr.in/) on understanding the roles of attention and MLP components in LLMs at a domain level. ([Preprint](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PC4Vs4XI6fRZt_iHqw0NWSWsrwiHWPB9/view?usp=sharing)).  
We initially began by searching for domain-specific circuits. ([Project Website](https://inside-llms.gitbook.io/surgical-domain-discovery)).

Other projects & explorations: 
As a curious starter, I’ve worked on exploratory projects including applying diffusion methods to structured physics data in [Sparse diffusion models (CERN)](https://github.com/ojasvanema/Sparse-Diffusion-Learning-Latent-Distribution-in-High-Energy-Physics), diffusion models for personalized generation in [Image Alchemy](https://openreview.net/forum?id=wOh5cAM9qC) and [low-resolution media forensics](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025W/APAI/html/Sharma_Explainable_AI-Generated_Image_Forensics_A_Low-Resolution_Perspective_with_Novel_Artifact_ICCVW_2025_paper.html).

Other than research and acads, I am fond of good music. To take a break, I sing and play the piano. I'm in the swimming team at IITR and love travelling around!
